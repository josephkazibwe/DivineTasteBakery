“Divine Sweetness in Every Bite”

Becky, CEO and founder of Divine Taste Bakery, once imagined her future among books. Her baking journey began with cookies and a few not-so-good cakes. During a holiday, with time on her hands, she decided to follow her growing interest and joined baking classes.

There, she discovered that success can come through many kinds of work—not only traditional office careers. She found joy in the flour, the learning and the chance to make people happy. Baking became a way to bless her family and bring celebrations and memorable moments to life for others.

Becky loves sharing that joy with every customer. She hopes to study at a culinary school, explore food beyond cakes, and one day share what she learns with others. She is determined to work hard and encourage people to pursue their dreams—and gives thanks to the Lord, whose help made this journey possible.
